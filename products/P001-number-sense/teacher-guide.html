TEACHER GUIDE

Five focused minutes a day.

This resource provides 30 short kindergarten number-sense practice pages with a familiar four-part routine.

Skills

Numerals 0–20 • counting • quantity matching • number order • comparison • visual representation • cumulative review.

Use it for

Morning work, math warm-ups, centers, intervention, homework, review or independent practice.

Printing

Designed for US Letter. Print in color or grayscale; task meaning never depends on color alone.

Terms

For use by the purchasing teacher/classroom. Do not redistribute, resell, or upload the resource publicly.
